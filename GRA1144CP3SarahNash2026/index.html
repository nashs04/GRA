From nightmares to reality

Six teenagers have moved from different places to the same neighborhood on the same week. Each of them have struggles of their own. Adrien wishes his self-esteem is higher. Bexleigh is very secretive due to being extremely afraid of how her classmates would react if she is more extroverted. Celeste wants to be seen as more than just her many personal issues. Donovan’s memories as a former bully continue to haunt him. Everest has not been social in years. Finch has a love-hate relationship with the idea of being a sketch artist, a detective, and a monster specialist. Shortly after they are given an ordinary start as new students in a local school, a supernatural being is rumored to be found hovering over the town. As more surreal events pile up, Finch calls upon Everest, Donovan, Celeste, Bexleigh, and Adrien to join in on the investigation. Will the group prove themselves to be worth honoring or will they fail to face their biggest fears?
