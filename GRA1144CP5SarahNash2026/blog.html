The Story Behind Neon Fatigue

In the silkscreen class I took in Spring 2024, there was an assignment in which I needed to come up with a poster design for either a movie, theater stage play, or a musician/band tour. Given that I am not used to using stuff that exists in real life, I tend to come up with fictional stuff for those assignments. I developed this habit because I hate when something is chosen for me. Because I couldn’t decide the kind of subject matter to be depicted on the poster, I decided to look at a family member of mine. I have a brother who is very into 1990s and early-to-mid 2000s rock music, and he claimed to be born in the wrong generation. At the time when the assignment was in its early stages, he was forming a band with at least two or three other people. The band pictures I used in the first draft and later the final submission were a mix of my own drawings and stock photos. I named my fictional band Neon Fatigue after questioning myself, “What kind of name that isn’t taken by a real-life band is typical for certain genres?” Not only did create a fictional band to use as the topic, but I also put in fictional locations in one of the assignment’s required areas.

The differences between the Neon Fatigue in the present day and the Neon Fatigue in the past are deliberate. A few months after I came up with the idea, the real-life band it was based on broke up and disbanded. When I revisited Neon Fatigue, I thought that if I constantly base a work of fiction off something I am not as interested in as I used to be, it will flatten itself into a nothingburger. And so, I decided to cut away most of Neon Fatigue’s roots in exchange for others that are somewhat aligned with my personal tastes. This includes changing the appearances of the band members and adding new members to Neon Fatigue.

When it came to redesigning the logo to go with the Neon Fatigue’s rebrand, I considered changing it to its initials, but then I changed my mind as there is already a living musician with the NF initials. Then I decided to do a twist on this trend by emphasizing the initials and using another font to cut out some fragments in the letters. As of this writing, I am still finalizing this website’s appearance. Most of the images here are placeholders because I am still putting together the visuals.
